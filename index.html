About

End-to-end data work, with a soft spot for shipping.

I'm a Data Engineer at Amentum with four years of experience across IT, automation, and data work. Most of my projects involve SQL, dashboards, internal tools, and finding better ways to handle messy operational processes.

I enjoy the side of data engineering that connects everything together. Pulling data from APIs, cleaning it in Python, modeling it in SQL Server or Postgres, and turning it into dashboards people can actually use day to day.

A lot of my work also drifts into full-stack development. I like building internal tools that make life easier for the teams using them, not just the engineers maintaining them.

Outside of work, I built and launched my own mobile app on the App Store and Google Play, handling the frontend, backend, database, analytics, and deployment myself. Those projects are my favorite because I love being involved in the whole process from idea to launch.
